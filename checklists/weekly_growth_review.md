# Weekly Growth Review

Use this before office hours. Growth should be measured with the best real behavioral metric available.

## Metric

- What was the key metric last week?
- What is it this week?
- What is the weekly growth rate?
- Why is this metric real rather than vanity?
- If the metric moved, which users/accounts caused the movement?
- If the metric did not move, where is the bottleneck?

## Funnel

- Where did prospects/users enter?
- Where did they drop?
- Where did they reach first value?
- Where did they return, repeat, or pay?
- Which segment performed best?
- Which segment should we stop serving for now?

## User learning

- What surprised us?
- What objection repeated?
- What workaround did we discover?
- Who is the most excited user and why?
- Who seemed interested but took no action?
