# User Interview Script

## Before the call

Hypothesis:

- Problem:

- Why they might care:

## Opening

Thanks for taking the time. I am trying to understand how you currently handle [problem/workflow]. I am not here to pitch at first; I want to understand what actually happens today.

## Questions

1. Tell me about the last time this problem happened.
2. What triggered it?
3. What did you do?
4. What tools/people/processes were involved?
5. What was annoying, expensive, slow, risky, or embarrassing?
6. How often does this happen?
7. What happens if you do nothing?
8. Have you tried to solve it before?
9. Who else cares about this?
10. What would make you switch from the current workaround?

## If showing product

- Watch silently first.
- Ask: what is confusing?
- Ask: what would you expect next?
- Ask: would you use this for a real task now?
- Ask: what would stop you?

## Closing

- Can I follow up after I change the product?
- Who else should I talk to?
- Would you be willing to try this on a real workflow?
