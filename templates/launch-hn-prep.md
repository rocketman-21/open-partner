Sources:

## Launch Readiness

Can readers try the product directly?

If not, is there a compelling demo?

Is pricing clear enough for launch-day readers?

Can the team spend the day answering comments?

Is the product mature enough that the discussion will teach us something useful?

## Tone Checks

- Factual, personal, and modest.
- Not salesy.
- Not investor-pitch language.
- No exaggerated superlatives.
- Technical details included where useful.
- Reader treated as a peer.

## Comment-Day Rules

- Reply steadily.
- Be generous with details.
- Treat criticism as useful.
- Do not ask friends to post booster comments.
- Optimize for the silent audience, not for winning arguments.
