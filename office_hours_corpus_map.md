## Session source order

Use this order unless the situation clearly demands otherwise:

1. **Facts and metrics:** `templates/weekly-metrics.md`, `templates/oh_prep.md`.
2. **YC office-hours method:** `pg_essays/what_ive_learned_from_users.md`, `yc_program/office_hours_model.md`.
3. **Growth/user doctrine:** `pg_essays/startup_equals_growth.md`, `pg_essays/do_things_that_dont_scale.md`.
4. **Problem-specific source:** choose from `sources/pg_yc_office_hours_source_matrix.md`.
5. **Action template:** `templates/experiment_card.md` or `templates/session-summary.md`.

## Common misroutes

- Do not use fundraising sources when the real issue is weak user love.
- Do not use launch sources when the real issue is manual acquisition.
- Do not use hiring/team sources when the founders have not personally done the sales/user work.
- Do not use product-roadmap sources when activation/retention has not been observed with real users.
- Do not use crypto community sources without translating them into behavior, revenue, or retained usage.
