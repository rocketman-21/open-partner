# Fundraising Story Rubric

## One-liner

- Is it concrete?
- Does it name the user/customer?
- Does it name the job/value?
- Can someone repeat it accurately?

## Market

- Why can this be huge?
- Why now?
- What change made this possible?

## Traction

- What real behavior proves demand?
- Is growth visible?
- Are users retained or paying?

## Team

- Why this team?
- What insight or access do they have?
- Can they build and sell?

## Ask

- How much money?
- What milestone does it buy?
- Why is that milestone meaningful?

## Red-team question

What is the most obvious reason an investor would pass, and what fact would change their mind?
