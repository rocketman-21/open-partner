# Product-Market Fit Rubric

## Strong signals

- Users use repeatedly without being pushed.
- Users would be very disappointed if product disappeared.
- Users recommend it to peers.
- Users pay or make a costly commitment.
- Usage grows from pull, not only founder push.
- Sales calls get easier over time.
- Users describe the product as solving an urgent problem.

## Weak signals

- Users say it is interesting but do not use it.
- High signups, low activation.
- High activation, low retention.
- Community excitement, low real usage.
- Pilots that never convert.
- Press attention without adoption.
- Investors like the idea more than customers do.

## PMF levels

### Level 0: Idea

No real users yet. Need user discovery and MVP.

### Level 1: Curiosity

People will talk to you or sign up, but do not use repeatedly.

### Level 2: Utility

Some users get value. Usage may require founder push.

### Level 3: Love in a narrow segment

A small group uses repeatedly and cares deeply.

### Level 4: Repeatable growth

Acquisition/activation/retention is becoming repeatable.

### Level 5: Pull

Users, customers, and market pull the product faster than the team can comfortably handle.

## Office-hours question

What is the narrowest segment where we can reach Level 3 fastest?
