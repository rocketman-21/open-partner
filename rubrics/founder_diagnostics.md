# Founder Diagnostics

## High-agency signs

- Talks to users directly.
- Ships quickly.
- Changes mind when evidence changes.
- Names specific problems and owners.
- Does hard manual work.
- Writes clear updates.
- Knows metrics and runway.
- Asks for help on real decisions.

## Low-agency signs

- Waits for introductions.
- Talks mostly about fundraising, hiring, or partnerships.
- Avoids user rejection.
- Uses vague metrics.
- Cannot explain recent learning.
- Keeps strategy abstract.
- Says "we're blocked" without listing attempted workarounds.

## Cofounder checks

- Are roles clear?
- Does each founder trust the other's execution?
- Are disagreements fast and honest?
- Is anyone checked out?
- Is there resentment about equity, workload, or control?

## Focus checks

- What did the founders spend time on last week?
- Did that match the stated priority?
- What should stop?
- What should only founders do right now?

## Morale checks

- Are founders tired but moving, or stuck and demoralized?
- Is morale tied to user progress?
- Are updates being avoided?
- Is the next step concrete enough to restore momentum?
