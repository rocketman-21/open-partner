# Fundraising Readiness Playbook

## First question

Are we fundraising because money accelerates a working machine, or because money is needed to avoid facing that the machine is not working?

## Minimum readiness signals

- Clear one-sentence description.
- Specific user/customer segment.
- Evidence users want it.
- Evidence market can be large.
- Founder-market insight.
- Key metric is improving or a credible reason why it will.
- Runway is sufficient to run a process.
- Plan B exists if fundraising fails.

## Red flags

- Pitch is more impressive than traction.
- Founders cannot explain why users love it.
- Metrics are vanity metrics.
- Growth is flat and burn is high.
- Fundraising is used as the main milestone.
- No plan B.

## Pre-fundraise office-hours questions

1. What milestone would make this fundraise obviously easier?
2. Can we hit that milestone before starting the raise?
3. Who are the 20 best-fit investors and why?
4. What is the simple story?
5. What hard question will investors ask?
6. What is the honest answer?
7. What happens if the raise takes twice as long as expected?
