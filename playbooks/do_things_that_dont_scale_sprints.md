# Do-Things-That-Don't-Scale Sprint Menu

Pick one per week unless there is a crisis.

## Sprint A: 10 obsessed users

Goal: find 10 people with intense need.

Actions:

- List 50 targets.
- Contact 30.
- Book 10 calls/demos.
- Personally onboard 3-5.
- Ask what would make them love it.

Success:

- At least 3 users reach value.
- At least 1 asks for follow-up or introduces someone.

## Sprint B: Concierge product

Goal: learn the workflow before automating.

Actions:

- Pick one customer/user.
- Solve their problem manually.
- Track every manual step.
- Identify repeatable pieces.

Success:

- User gets real value.
- We know what to automate next.

## Sprint C: Contained fire

Goal: narrow the market until adoption is easier.

Actions:

- Choose one niche segment.
- Rewrite landing/pitch for them only.
- Contact 20 people in that niche.
- Track conversion and objections.

Success:

- Segment has higher response, activation, or retention than broader target.

## Sprint D: Founder-led sales

Goal: learn sales before hiring sales.

Actions:

- Founder runs 10 sales calls.
- Log objections.
- Ask for payment or concrete commitment.
- Follow up personally.

Success:

- Clear buying objections.
- Pricing insight.
- At least one paid/committed pilot or strong no.

## Sprint E: Delight loop

Goal: make early users unusually happy.

Actions:

- Pick top 5 users.
- Ask what is annoying/confusing.
- Fix one thing within 48 hours.
- Send personal update.

Success:

- User repeats usage, refers, pays, or shares deeper feedback.

## Sprint F: Churn autopsy

Goal: learn why users stop.

Actions:

- Contact 10 churned/inactive users.
- Ask what they expected and what failed.
- Classify reasons.
- Fix the highest-frequency issue.

Success:

- One clear retention bottleneck.

## Sprint G: Manual marketplace liquidity

Goal: make one side of a marketplace feel alive.

Actions:

- Manually match supply and demand.
- Curate options.
- Message participants directly.
- Track time-to-match and satisfaction.

Success:

- Repeat transaction or user asks for another match.
