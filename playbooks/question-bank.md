# Office-Hours Question Bank

Use these questions one at a time. Push for specific facts.

## Universal Opening

- What changed since last time?
- What did users do?
- What did users teach you?
- What is the biggest problem now?
- What are you tempted to work on that might be fake work?

## Demand

- Who would be genuinely upset if this disappeared?
- Who has paid, committed, returned, invited others, or built a workflow around it?
- What is the strongest evidence this is demand rather than interest?
- What is the current workaround?
- What does the workaround cost?

## User Specificity

- Name the exact user who needs this most.
- What is their role?
- What gets them promoted or fired?
- What do they do today at 3pm when this problem happens?
- Where can we find 20 more people like them?

## MVP

- What is the smallest thing that creates real utility?
- What can be manual?
- What can be ugly?
- What must be trustworthy?
- What can we ship this week?

## Activation

- What is the first value moment?
- What percentage of users reach it?
- Where do they drop off?
- Have you watched someone try it silently?
- What is the smallest fix that gets more users to value?

## Retention

- Who comes back?
- Who does not?
- What is different about them?
- What habit, workflow, or recurring pain should pull users back?
- What would users do if the product vanished?

## Growth

- What is the weekly growth rate?
- What caused this week's growth?
- Is growth from a repeatable loop or a one-time push?
- Is retention good enough to add more users?
- What is the next manual acquisition move?

## Sales

- Who is the buyer?
- Who is the user?
- Who blocks the deal?
- What is the next concrete step?
- What did the prospect do after the call?
- What objection repeats?

## Pricing

- What value does the customer get?
- What budget does this come from?
- What do they pay for the workaround?
- Have they paid anything yet?
- What price would make them take the product seriously?

## Fundraising

- Are we in fundraising mode?
- Why now?
- What proof do we have?
- Are we default alive or default dead?
- What is plan B if the round fails?
- Which investor has the highest expected value?
- What is the next concrete action for each investor?

## Runway

- How much cash is left?
- What is monthly burn?
- How many months of runway remain?
- What spend creates learning or growth?
- What spend is comfort, optics, or inertia?
- What is the survival plan?

## Hiring

- What repeatable job are we hiring for?
- Have founders done this job manually?
- What breaks if we do not hire?
- What breaks if we do hire?
- Does this hire increase speed or coordination overhead?

## Founder Team

- What hard conversation is being avoided?
- Are responsibilities clear?
- Who owns this cycle's biggest problem?
- Where are incentives or expectations misaligned?
- What decision needs to be made explicitly?

## Closing

- What is the one priority?
- What are we not doing?
- What is the experiment?
- What metric decides success?
- When do we review?
