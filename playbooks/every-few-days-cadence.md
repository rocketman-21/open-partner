# Every-Few-Days Office-Hours Cadence

This cadence is designed for founder + Codex sessions every 2-3 days.

## Why Short Cadence Works

Early startups need fast correction. A short cadence forces:

- Specific commitments.
- Fresh user contact.
- Fast learning.
- Less room for fake work.
- Smaller experiments.

## Cadence Template

### Day 0: Full Office Hours

Length: 45 minutes.

Outputs:

- Diagnosis.
- One priority.
- Experiment.
- Metrics to report back.

### Day 2 or 3: Checkpoint

Length: 20 minutes.

Questions:

- Did the action happen?
- What did users do?
- What did we learn?
- Is the priority still correct?
- What is the next action?

### Day 5 or 7: Weekly Review

Length: 60 minutes.

Questions:

- What changed in metrics?
- What changed in user understanding?
- Which hypothesis survived?
- What should we stop doing?
- What gets the next week?

## Standing Rules

- No session without user or market evidence unless the company is blocked before reaching users.
- No more than one main priority.
- No action item without an owner and review date.
- No growth push before retention unless the goal is to gather more learning.
- No fundraising drift unless explicitly in fundraising mode.
