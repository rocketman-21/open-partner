# 12-Week Mock YC Batch Plan

## Week 0: Baseline

- Fill company snapshot.
- Define key metric.
- Calculate runway and default-alive/dead.
- List current users/customers.
- List top 3 existential risks.

## Week 1: User reality

- 10 user/customer conversations.
- Update ICP.
- Identify strongest pain.

## Week 2: MVP / value moment

- Define smallest useful product.
- Reduce time-to-value.
- Launch/relaunch to narrow segment.

## Week 3: Do things that don't scale

- Manual acquisition sprint.
- Concierge onboarding.
- Delight top users.

## Week 4: Growth bottleneck

- Funnel review.
- Pick growth metric.
- Run acquisition/activation experiment.

## Week 5: Retention

- Cohort/usage review.
- Churn interviews.
- Fix one retention issue.

## Week 6: Revenue/pricing

- Founder-led sales.
- Pricing test.
- Paid pilot/commitment push.

## Week 7: Focus reset

- Stop low-value work.
- Re-rank problems.
- Decide if pivot/narrowing is needed.

## Week 8: Fundraising readiness

- Default-alive/dead analysis.
- Investor narrative draft.
- Decide whether to fundraise now or hit another milestone.

## Week 9: Launch/visibility

- Narrow launch plan.
- User/community/press targets.
- Measure activation, not attention.

## Week 10: Hiring/team

- Decide what founders must keep doing.
- Identify true hiring bottlenecks.
- Cofounder/team alignment review.

## Week 11: Demo Day-style narrative

- 2-minute pitch.
- Metrics proof.
- User story.
- Ask.

## Week 12: Batch retrospective

- What improved?
- What did users teach us?
- What is still fatal?
- What is next 12-week goal?
