## Notes On Access

- Some YC community and company-network surfaces are private or require login. This corpus should describe their public mechanics rather than attempting to scrape private content.
- Public pages can change. Re-check source URLs when using this corpus for current program terms or application decisions.
- Fundraising/legal materials are for research only. Consult qualified counsel before using SAFE documents or fundraising protocols.
