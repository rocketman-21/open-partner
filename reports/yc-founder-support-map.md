## Support Mechanisms

| Mechanism | What YC Provides | Founder Benefit | Mock Version |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Capital | Standard investment on YC terms. | Runway and credibility. | Runway review and capital-use discipline. |
| Time-boxed batch | 3-month program, intense pre-Demo Day period. | Urgency, focus, and pace. | 2-3 day office-hours cycle plus weekly review. |
| Groups | Batch sorted into partner-led groups. | Partner familiarity and pattern recognition. | Persistent company memo and recurring diagnosis. |
| Sections | Small groups of 6-10 companies. | Candid peer pressure and intimate comparison set. | Small benchmark set or invited peer review later. |
| One-on-one office hours | Partner time as often as founders want. | Individualized advice and root-cause diagnosis. | Mock office hours on demand with templates. |
| Group office hours | Recurring group advice. | Learn from other founders' problems. | Problem-pattern reviews and anonymized case studies. |
| Alumni talks | Off-record early stories from successful founders. | Calibration and honest examples. | Case-study notes from public interviews and YC content. |
| Public launch help | Launch YC, HN/Product Hunt/press prep. | First users, feedback, attention. | Launch checklist, launch copy, manual follow-up plan. |
| First customers | YC community can supply early customers. | Smart initial feedback and early revenue. | Manual customer map and warm-intro plan. |
| Demo Day | Concentrated investor audience. | Fundraising momentum and price competition. | Fundraising mode, investor pipeline, and pitch review. |
| Post-Demo Day fundraising help | Decode investor signals and process. | Avoid ambiguity and close rounds faster. | Investor pipeline tracker and handshake discipline. |
| Bookface | Founder network and expert lookup. | Fast answers, intros, peer knowledge. | Ask map: who can answer this exact problem? |
| Alumni community | Ongoing partner/alumni access. | Long-term support beyond batch. | Standing advisor and peer list. |
| Deals | Discounts/free accounts. | Lower burn and less setup friction. | Startup stack audit and burn reduction. |
| Brand | YC credibility. | More attention from users, hires, investors, press. | Clear proof and positioning to substitute for brand. |
| Hiring | Work at a Startup, HN jobs, network. | First hires and senior talent. | Hiring readiness checklist and role definition. |
| Standard docs | SAFE documents and protocols. | Lower fundraising/legal friction. | Use standard docs with counsel; avoid custom process. |

## The Underlying Pattern

YC makes founders more effective by combining:

- Higher ambition.
- Higher urgency.
- Better diagnosis.
- Denser feedback.
- Better peer comparison.
- Better network access.
- Less fundraising friction.
- More accountability.

## What To Emulate First

Highest-leverage pieces for us:

1. User-learning discipline.
2. Weekly measurable progress.
3. Brutal priority selection.
4. Short experiments.
5. Fundraising mode discipline.
6. Founder-led sales and support.
7. Launch readiness and follow-up.
8. Written continuity across sessions.

## What Not To Fake

Do not pretend we have:

- YC investor access.
- YC brand.
- Bookface.
- Alumni network.
- Partner authority.

Instead, explicitly substitute:

- Better preparation.
- Better follow-through.
- More concrete customer work.
- Public evidence.
- Clearer writing.
- Warmer intros from real relationships.
