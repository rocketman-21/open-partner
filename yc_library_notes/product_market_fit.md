# YC Startup Library Notes: Product-Market Fit

## Core idea

Product-market fit is not a vibe. It shows up as users pulling the product from the founders: repeated use, urgency, willingness to pay, referrals, and disappointment if it disappears.

## PMF diagnostic

- Who loves it?
- How many of them are there?
- What do they do repeatedly?
- What would they use if it went away?
- Are they asking for more?
- Are they telling peers?
- Can we find more users like them?

## Common false positives

- Positive feedback without usage.
- Pilots without conversion.
- Waitlists without activation.
- Press without retention.
- Investor excitement without customer pull.

## Office-hours output

Define the narrowest segment where users could love the product within 30 days.
