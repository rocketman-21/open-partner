# YC Startup Library Notes: First Customers

## Core idea

The first customers are usually earned manually. Founder-led sales is a learning process, not just a revenue process.

## First-customer tactics

- Start with a narrow ICP.
- Use direct outreach.
- Ask for a real commitment.
- Personally onboard.
- Charge earlier than feels comfortable if B2B.
- Log objections.
- Iterate pitch and product after every call.

## Common mistakes

- Hiring sales too early.
- Waiting for inbound.
- Avoiding price conversations.
- Over-customizing without finding repeatability.
- Treating pilots as success when they do not convert.

## Office-hours questions

- Who are the next 20 prospects?
- What exact message will you send?
- What commitment are you asking for?
- What did the last 5 prospects object to?
- What did we change because of sales calls?
