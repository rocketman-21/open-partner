# YC Startup Library Notes: Cofounders and Team

## Core idea

Cofounder quality and alignment are among the most important early startup variables. Founder conflict can become a company-killing issue.

## Cofounder diagnostics

- Are roles explicit?
- Are expectations explicit?
- Is equity understood and accepted?
- Are decisions fast?
- Does each founder trust the other's effort and judgment?
- Are hard conversations happening early?

## Hiring principles

- Founders should do sales and customer learning before hiring someone to do it.
- Hire to remove proven bottlenecks, not to look like a real company.
- Overhiring can create default-dead risk.

## Office-hours questions

- What is the unresolved conversation between founders?
- What work should a founder still own?
- Which hire would directly accelerate the current bottleneck?
- What hire would merely make us feel better?
