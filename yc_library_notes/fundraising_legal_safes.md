# YC Startup Library Notes: Funding, SAFEs, Legal Mechanics

## Core idea

Financing mechanics matter, but they should serve the company rather than replace company-building. YC standardized the SAFE to reduce friction in early-stage fundraising.

## What founders need to know early

- How much money is needed to reach a meaningful milestone.
- What dilution a financing instrument implies.
- Whether the company is default alive or dead.
- Whether fundraising is plan A only, or whether plan B exists.
- Basic incorporation/IP/equity hygiene.

## What founders should not overfocus on early

- Complex financing optimization before traction.
- Legal edge cases unrelated to current stage.
- Fancy investor process before a credible story.

## Office-hours questions

- What milestone does this money buy?
- How does this round change the company's odds?
- What does the investor need to believe?
- What is the plan if the round fails?
