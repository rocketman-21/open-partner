# Startups in 13 Sentences — Checklist Version

Use this as a recurring diagnostic checklist.

## 1. Pick good cofounders

- Do the founders trust each other?
- Is there complementary execution ability?
- Can disagreements be resolved fast?

## 2. Launch fast

- Is something in users' hands?
- What has launch taught us?
- What are we delaying out of fear?

## 3. Let the idea evolve

- What did we change because of users?
- Are we defending the original idea too much?

## 4. Understand your users

- What do users want, fear, and currently do?
- What surprised us in user conversations?

## 5. Better to make a few users love you

- Who loves it now?
- What would make them more intense?
- Are we spreading ourselves across too many lukewarm users?

## 6. Offer surprisingly good customer service

- What high-touch support would big companies never do?
- What support interactions produce product insight?

## 7. You make what you measure

- What is on the wall/dashboard?
- Does it reflect real progress?

## 8. Spend little

- What expenses reduce optionality?
- What costs are just startup theater?

## 9. Get ramen profitable

- What revenue level covers founder living expenses?
- What is the fastest path there?

## 10. Avoid distractions

- Are consulting, conferences, fundraising, partnerships, or side projects stealing focus?

## 11. Do not get demoralized

- Is morale low because progress is low?
- What concrete action can create momentum?

## 12. Do not give up

- Are we quitting because the idea is bad, or because the emotional low is intense?

## 13. Deals are dynamic

- Are we treating investor/customer/partner negotiations as static when momentum can change them?
