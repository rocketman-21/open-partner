# How Not to Die — Survival Notes

## Core idea

Startups often die less dramatically than founders imagine. They drift, stop shipping, stop talking to people, stop updating advisors, and become demoralized.

## Warning signs

- No user conversations for a week.
- No product changes for a week.
- No measurable progress since last office hours.
- Founders avoid updates because there is nothing to say.
- Founders spend time on abstract strategy instead of concrete forward motion.
- Burn continues while learning slows.
- A cofounder relationship problem remains unspoken.

## Accountability principle

Regular contact creates deadlines. Deadlines create motion. Motion creates new facts. New facts reduce despair.

## Mock-office-hours rule

If there is no progress, the session should not become a long philosophical conversation. It should produce one concrete action within 24-72 hours.

## Rescue protocol

1. Identify whether the company is actually stuck or just emotionally low.
2. Force contact with users/customers.
3. Ship a small improvement.
4. Cut one distraction.
5. Send a written update.
6. Re-check in 48-72 hours.

## Founder morale diagnostic

- Are you tired because you are working hard, or demoralized because work is not turning into learning?
- Are you avoiding users because rejection hurts?
- Are you avoiding metrics because the numbers are bad?
- Are you avoiding your cofounder because the conversation is hard?

## Bias

When in doubt, keep typing: ship, sell, talk, learn.
