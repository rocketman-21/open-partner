# Case Study: Viaweb and Manual Work Behind the Product

## Why this matters

PG describes doing manual work for merchants as a way to understand what software needed to do. This pattern is central to early product discovery.

## Pattern

- Users may not adopt self-serve software immediately.
- Founders can do work on users' behalf.
- Manual execution reveals missing features.
- The founder develops "muscle memory" for the workflow before automating.

## Mock-office-hours questions

- What customer job can we do manually this week?
- What would we learn by doing it ourselves?
- Which manual step repeats enough to automate?
- Are we building automation before understanding the work?

## Action template

Manually complete [workflow] for [number] users, record every step, then automate only the repeated bottleneck.
