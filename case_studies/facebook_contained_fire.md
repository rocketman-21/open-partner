# Case Study: Facebook and Contained Fire

## Why this matters

A narrow initial market can be a strength. Facebook's early college-by-college expansion made the product feel native to each community before it expanded more broadly.

## Pattern

- Start with a segment where users know each other.
- Make the product feel specifically built for that segment.
- Achieve density before expansion.
- Expand from a hot core rather than a lukewarm broad launch.

## Mock-office-hours questions

- Which segment could reach critical mass fastest?
- Do users in the segment influence one another?
- What would make them feel "this is for people like us"?
- Are we prematurely broadening the product/brand?

## Action template

For the next sprint, ignore the broad market and optimize only for [specific segment/community].
