# Case Study: Wufoo and Early User Delight

## Why this matters

Wufoo is a classic example in the "do things that don't scale" doctrine: early users received unusually personal attention. The tactic matters because early delight can shape culture, retention, and feedback quality.

## Pattern

- Treat first users as precious.
- Give them more attention than a large company could.
- Use service interactions to learn what product should become.
- Make the user's decision to try you feel obviously good.

## Mock-office-hours questions

- What would make our first users feel surprisingly cared for?
- What can we do that a scaled incumbent would never do?
- Which user support moments are actually product discovery moments?
- Are we trying to look professional by being distant?

## Action template

Pick the top 5 early users and do one personal, high-effort thing for each that improves their outcome and teaches us something.
