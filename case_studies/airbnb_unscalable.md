# Case Study: Airbnb and Heroic Early User Work

## Why this matters

Airbnb is the canonical YC/PG example of an early company that looked fragile but survived because the founders did laborious, manual, non-scalable work.

## Pattern

- Marketplace problem: hard to get both supply and demand moving.
- Narrow geography: New York was a concentrated market.
- Founder action: went to users in person, recruited hosts, helped improve listings, and took better photos.
- Learning loop: every in-person interaction taught them what hosts and guests needed.
- Office-hours lesson: do not judge a larval startup by how small it looks. Ask how big it could become if founders do the right things.

## Mock-office-hours questions

- What is our New York?
- Who are the users we should physically/virtually visit?
- What would we do for them manually if we were desperate to make them successful?
- What user-side improvement would make the marketplace/product feel dramatically better?
- What part of our product can we learn only by watching users use it?

## Action template

This week we will personally onboard/help [number] users in [narrow segment], and we will improve [specific asset/workflow] for them by hand.
