# YC Application + Interview Criteria — Practical Notes

## What YC appears to reward

- Clear, concise explanation.
- Concrete product description.
- Strong founders.
- Evidence of user insight.
- Progress since starting or since last application.
- A potentially huge market.
- A team that can build the product itself.
- Willingness to apply early, not wait for perfect traction.

## Application clarity test

After reading your first sentence, can a tired reviewer reproduce what you are building?

Bad pattern:

- Abstract market language.
- Grand claims without specifics.
- Jargon that could describe many startups.

Better pattern:

- We build X for Y so they can Z.
- Current alternative is A; our wedge is B.
- We have C users/customers and learned D.

## Interview-style pressure questions

- What exactly are you building?
- Who uses it?
- Why do they need it now?
- What do users do today instead?
- How many users/customers do you have?
- What did you learn from them?
- What is growing?
- Why is this market big?
- Why are you the team to win?
- What is the hardest unsolved problem?
- What would make this fail?

## Mock-office-hours use

We should periodically write your company in YC-application form. If it is hard to explain, the problem might be product clarity, market clarity, or lack of user learning.
