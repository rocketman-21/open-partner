# Startup School — Adaptation Notes

## What Startup School is

Startup School is YC's free online course/resource for founders. It provides essential startup advice, accountability for weekly progress, and access to co-founder matching.

## Current curriculum themes

- How to plan an MVP
- Modern startup funding
- How to launch in the press
- How to get users and grow
- How to lead teams
- How and when to apply to YC
- Founder stories and scaling case studies
- Co-founder matching
- Weekly update/progress tracking

## How to adapt it for our mock YC loop

### Week 1: Company snapshot + MVP clarity

- Who is the user?
- What is the smallest useful product?
- What is already launched?

### Week 2: User conversations

- Talk to users.
- Capture jobs/pains/workarounds.
- Update ICP and product assumptions.

### Week 3: Launch/relaunch

- Launch to a narrow segment.
- Avoid big-launch theater.
- Use launch as a learning event.

### Week 4: First customers

- Founder-led sales.
- Manual onboarding.
- Pricing test.

### Week 5: Growth and metrics

- Pick one or two key metrics.
- Remove vanity metrics.
- Find bottleneck.

### Week 6: Funding/runway

- Default alive/dead.
- Fundraising readiness.
- Plan B if financing fails.

### Week 7: Leadership/team

- Cofounder alignment.
- Hiring only if bottleneck requires it.
- Founder productivity.

## Standing assignment

Every week, answer: what did users teach us that changed what we will do next?
