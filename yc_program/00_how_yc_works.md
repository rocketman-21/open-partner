# How YC Works — Current Program Model

## Current program structure

YC is a three-month startup program that runs four times a year: winter, spring, summer, and fall. The stated goal is to help startups be in dramatically better shape after the batch, usually via a better product, more users, and more fundraising options.

## Funding

YC's standard deal is $500,000:

- $125,000 on a post-money SAFE for 7%.
- $375,000 on an uncapped MFN SAFE.

## Batch structure

- Startups are sorted into groups.
- Groups are led by YC partners.
- Groups are split into smaller sections of roughly 6-10 companies.
- Founders get one-on-one office hours and group office hours.
- There is an in-person kickoff and regular San Francisco meetups.

## Program resources

- Partner office hours.
- Batchmates and sections.
- Weekly meetups and talks from experienced founders.
- Bookface/community knowledge base.
- Help with launches.
- Potential first customers from the YC community.
- Demo Day and investor network access.
- Ongoing office hours after the batch.
- Alumni community and topic-specific founder communities.

## What to copy for mock office hours

- Small-group accountability.
- Dedicated partner/context memory.
- Weekly measurable progress.
- Real founder/user problems rather than abstract curriculum.
- Short-cycle experiments.
- Written updates.

## What cannot be copied directly

- YC's brand signal.
- Investor concentration at Demo Day.
- Bookface/alumni network scale.
- Dense in-person batch effects.
