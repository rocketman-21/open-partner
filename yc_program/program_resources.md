# YC Program Resources — What They Do for Startups

## Funding

YC provides standardized early capital. This helps founders focus and reduces fundraising complexity at the earliest stage.

## Advice

Dedicated partners advise founders during the batch and through ongoing office hours after the batch.

## Groups and sections

Small groups and sections create intimacy inside a large batch. Founders get peer pressure, tactical advice, and comparison points.

## Bookface/community

YC's internal platform and alumni network let founders ask questions, get introductions, find experts, and learn from previous founders.

## Weekly meetups and talks

Founders hear candid stories from successful founders and industry leaders. The deeper value is calibration: seeing what early-stage success and chaos actually looked like.

## Launch help

YC helps companies present launches to users, communities, and press. The goal is not vanity publicity; it is to get real users and feedback.

## First customers

B2B and consumer companies may get early customers or feedback from the YC community. This is especially useful because YC founders are sophisticated early adopters.

## Demo Day and fundraising

Demo Day concentrates investor attention. YC also helps founders interpret investor feedback and negotiate the fundraising process.

## Post-batch support

YC continues after the three-month program via ongoing office hours and the alumni network.

## What to emulate

- Write updates.
- Create a small trusted peer/advisor network.
- Build a living expert map.
- Use launches as learning events.
- Treat investor conversations as feedback but not truth.
- Keep returning to users and growth.
